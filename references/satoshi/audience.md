# Satoshi × men's health Instagram audience

## Who the Reel is for

Men (and partners who watch with them) who already half-follow testosterone, fertility, TRT discourse, sleep/performance, longevity, and biotech headlines — and who feel sold-to by both clinics and influencers.

They want:

- a **personally consequential** question (what does this claim do to *my* body / wallet / risk?),
- someone who **spots the incentive**,
- a **receipt** (or an honest “we don’t have one yet”),
- and enough humor that the cynicism feels sharp, not sour.

They do **not** want: medical advice cosplay, miracle protocols, patient-punching jokes, or conspiracy certainty.

## Format that travels on Instagram

- Vertical Reel, spoken host (Runway Act Two on a Peloton/film plate).
- Hook in the first 1–2 seconds; one thesis; one analogy max; one concrete receipt; provisional close.
- Caption stays short; screening happens later in the IG library after auto-publish.

## What “polished” means here

Conversation → structured brief (`chat_request.py`) → researched Satoshi script (`SATOSHI_PERSONA.md`) → speech + plate → Remotion → R2 → Graph publish to `@byoticallc`. No silent topic swap. Writing-contract hash invalidates stale drafts when the persona changes.
