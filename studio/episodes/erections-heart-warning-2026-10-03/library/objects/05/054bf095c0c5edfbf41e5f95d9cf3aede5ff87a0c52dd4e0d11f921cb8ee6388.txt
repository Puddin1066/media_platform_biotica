Your body has a dashboard. Apparently, it installed one warning light in a very inconvenient place. Naturally, the modern response is tape over the light, then buy powders, pills, gummies, and confidence-branded moon dust. But erections depend partly on blood flow. Persistent ED is associated with higher cardiovascular and atherosclerotic risk. Stress, anxiety, medicines, diabetes, and vascular issues can all contribute. Persistent ED can have many causes and does not diagnose heart disease, but it can be a reason to review cardiovascular risk. So skip the amateur detective work. Check blood pressure, cholesterol, blood sugar, medications, stress—the boring grown-up maintenance list. A dashboard light is not the engine failing. It is the engine refusing to let you pretend the dashboard is decorative.
