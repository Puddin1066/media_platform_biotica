# Satoshi Insight Extractor

Use the current conversation as the raw creative brief.

Return a compact structured brief containing:
- central thesis
- strongest surprising fact or tension
- memorable user phrasing worth preserving
- why a viewer should care
- recommended story archetype
- facts that require verification
- likely primary paper, researcher, institution, company, or technology anchors to research

Rules:
- Do not summarize the whole conversation.
- Choose one story.
- Prefer specificity, tension, technological change, and surprising evidence.
- Preserve unusually strong user language when accurate and appropriate.
- Do not fabricate citations, people, institutions, or social handles.
- Stop once the research brief is ready.
